Capabilities

Built for close reading of data

Tools designed to keep raw numbers, visual plots, and written interpretation in the same frame.

Local CSV Ingestion

Drop a .csv file or paste tabular text. Headers, numeric columns, and summary counts are inferred immediately in the browser.

Spatial Context Selection

Drag across cells or click row and column headers to scope your question. Instant aggregates (count, sum, mean, min, max) appear in the toolbar as you select.

Interactive Charts

Generate bar, line, area, scatter, and pie charts directly from a table selection or ask the sidekick to plot a comparison for you.

Inline Analyst Sidekick

Ask questions in plain language alongside your grid. Receive structured breakdowns, outlier checks, and embedded charts without leaving the table.

Persistent Workspaces

Sign in to keep your uploaded datasets and conversation threads organized in a session history sidebar you can return to anytime.

Legible by Default

High-contrast dark surfaces, tabular numerals, and clear typographic hierarchy keep long analysis sessions calm on the eyes.
